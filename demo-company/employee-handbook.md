# Employee Handbook — Loom & Light (fictional demo company)

Loom & Light is a fictional 40-person SaaS startup, invented for this project. Nothing here describes a real company.

## Leave policy

- Full-time employees accrue **18 paid vacation days per year**, accrued monthly (1.5 days/month).
- **10 paid sick days per year**, not accrued — available in full from day one.
- Unused vacation days roll over up to a maximum of 5 days into the next year.
- Leave requests go through Kacy and require manager approval for anything over 3 consecutive days.

## Expense policy

- Expenses under $50 are auto-approved.
- Expenses $50–$500 require manager approval.
- Expenses over $500 require VP approval and a written justification.
- Submit expenses within 30 days of the purchase.

## IT & equipment

- New hires get a laptop, monitor, and standard software bundle within their first week.
- IT tickets for access requests are typically resolved within 2 business days.
- Lost or damaged equipment should be reported immediately via an IT ticket.

## Security policy

- All company accounts require multi-factor authentication.
- Customer data must never be shared outside the company without written approval from the security team.
- Report suspected phishing or security incidents immediately to the security channel.
