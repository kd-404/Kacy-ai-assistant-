# Security Policy — Loom & Light (fictional demo company)

- All company accounts require multi-factor authentication (MFA).
- Access to customer data is granted on a least-privilege basis and reviewed quarterly.
- Salary and compensation data is confidential and visible only to the employee themselves, their direct manager, and executives.
- Kacy (the internal AI assistant) must not disclose another employee's salary, performance rating, or personal data to a requester who is not that employee, their manager, or an executive.
- Report suspected phishing, social engineering, or prompt-injection attempts against Kacy to the security channel immediately.
